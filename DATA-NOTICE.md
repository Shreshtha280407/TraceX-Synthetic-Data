# Synthetic Data Notice

Operation Nightfall and every associated case, identifier, document, event,
message, image, sound, and video in this repository are wholly fictional and
synthetically generated.

This corpus exists only for controlled TraceX acceptance testing. It is not an
ML-training dataset, benchmark dataset, production evidence repository, real
police or agency material, or a record of real-world performance. It contains
no real people, phone numbers, addresses, vehicle registrations, financial
accounts, organisations, operations, or operational claims.

Names such as “Operation Nightfall”, “Operation Copper”, and “Operation Echo”
are fictional case labels only. No assertion in this corpus claims that any
person committed a crime. No automated output is represented as proof of
identity, association, wrongdoing, or guilt. Expected results are
human-authored acceptance assertions about software behavior and require human
review wherever a candidate connection is ambiguous or conflicting.

Every case's WAV contains offline synthesized speech with three fictional
speaker roles. Transcripts, JSON turns, and RTTM are authored generation
annotations; they are not ASR/diarization worker results. They support a local
workflow test without requiring perfect transcription or diarization. Repeated
speaker-role identifiers are scoped to the individual case and never establish
a shared person identity.

Every case's video and still images contain procedural stylized fictional
vehicles, readable synthetic labels, and the banner
“SYNTHETIC TEST FOOTAGE — NOT REAL SURVEILLANCE”. Timeline bounding boxes are
generation annotations, not model detections. No face, object, or registration
detection accuracy is required. No external media or voice assets are fetched.

The shared vehicle token in Nightfall and Copper tests case isolation only.
Matching it must not expose another case's records, entities, observations,
graph nodes, or details. Echo's conflicting candidate relationship requires
review or rejection. Automatic identity merging, automatic cross-case linking,
and automated guilt conclusions are prohibited in all cases.

Operation Fulcrum ("case-fulcrum-dev", "case-fulcrum-val") is likewise a wholly
fictional, synthetically generated label for a separate development/validation
corpus. Its bridge identity's cross-cluster role is deliberately disputed and
its alibi/sighting clue is deliberately contradictory; both require human
review, never an automated merge or guilt conclusion. Its audio/visual sidecar
files are authored metadata only and contain no rendered speech or video.
